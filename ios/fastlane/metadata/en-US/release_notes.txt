A big update: hardware wallets, new Solana tools, and a redesigned wallet.

HARDWARE WALLETS
- Ledger support, including firmware updates, genuine check, and app management.
- CatCard smart-card wallets over USB.
- Air-gapped signers via animated QR codes.

NEW SOLANA TOOLS
- Bulk Sell: sell several tokens for SOL at once.
- Reclaim SOL: close empty token accounts and recover their rent.
- My Staked: all your ChiefStaker positions in one place.
- Verified badges on known tokens.

WALLET
- Accounts grouped by wallet, with options to show or hide them.
- Step-by-step guide for creating an MPC wallet.
- Improved Bitcoin and EVM account handling.
- Send to your other wallets' accounts in one tap.
- Swap shows balances on both sides, with more accurate MAX quotes.
- Profit and loss view, and a redesigned activity history.
- Optional app lock with your iPhone passcode.

ALSO
- Available in French, Japanese, and Portuguese.
- Requires iOS 15 or later.
- Many design improvements and bug fixes.

Tibane gives Solana power users a single, fast app for everything that matters: a non-custodial wallet, token swaps, a staking explorer with live pool stats, tools to clean up your wallet and reclaim SOL, and a token info tool with on-chain holders, market cap, and supply.

WALLET
- Non-custodial. You stay in control of your keys.
- Create an MPC wallet with guided setup, or use a classic recovery phrase.
- Hardware wallets: Ledger, CatCard, and air-gapped QR signers.
- Solana first, with Bitcoin and EVM accounts in the same app.
- Live balances, portfolio totals, profit and loss, and full activity history.
- Verified badges on known tokens.
- Optional app lock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your iPhone passcode.

SWAP AND BULK SELL
- Swap Solana tokens at the best available quote.
- Bulk Sell: sell several tokens for SOL in a single flow.

STAKING POOLS
- Browse every ChiefStaker pool: total staked, rewards, member count.
- Dive into a pool's full stats: pool weight, decay (tau), milestones.
- My Staked: all your positions and pending rewards in one place, with one-tap Claim All.

CLEAN UP AND RECLAIM SOL
- Reclaim SOL: close empty token accounts and get their rent back (about 0.002 SOL each).
- Incinerator: burn junk tokens, NFTs, and unused .sol domains.
- Sponsored mode: a relay pays the network fee, with an adjustable tip.

TOKEN INFO
- Look up any SPL token: supply, market cap, burned amount, decimals.
- Top holders with on-chain percentages.
- Mint address with one-tap copy.

DAPP BROWSER
- Launch any Solana dApp from the in-app browser.
- Sign with the wallet you already have set up.

Available in English, French, Japanese, and Portuguese.

Open source. Built by Tibane Labs, a Solana research team named after the cat that started it all.
